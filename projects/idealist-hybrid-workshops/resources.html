Programme decision

Input: surveys and Facebook discussions highlighted practical questions from immigrant entrepreneurs.

Decision: use those audience signals to shape workshop content, including the topic “How to sell to Americans.”

Delivery: connect the learning focus with partner coordination and hybrid participation.
