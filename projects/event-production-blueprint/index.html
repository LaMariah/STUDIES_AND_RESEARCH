REUSABLE FRAMEWORK

Work backwards from opening day

  1. Briefdefine the audience, outcome and budget ceiling
  2. Designmap the programme, guest journey and physical or digital spaces
  3. Buildassign owners and dates for suppliers, staffing, approvals and handoffs
  4. Deliverrun the show, escalate issues and log decisions
  5. Reviewcompare outcomes with goals and assign follow-up
