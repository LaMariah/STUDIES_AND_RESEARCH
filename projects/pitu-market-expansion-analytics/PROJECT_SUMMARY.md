# Project summary for recruiters

## One sentence

I helped translate an unfamiliar Brazilian beverage into a US market-entry campaign that connected consumer education, social performance, retail activation, and distributor growth.

## Interview version

The brand faced an education problem rather than a product-quality problem. Buyers did not see consumer demand because many consumers did not understand cachaça or the caipirinha. I helped structure a campaign that taught the cocktail first, supported retailers with tastings and recipe materials, and gave distributors evidence of growing interest. The portfolio analysis recreates the measurement framework with aggregate figures because the original client data is confidential.

- Audience segmentation
- B2B sales enablement
- Campaign measurement
- ROI modelling
- Cross-functional communication

The repository recreates the campaign methodology and reported aggregate outcomes. It contains no confidential client or consumer data.
