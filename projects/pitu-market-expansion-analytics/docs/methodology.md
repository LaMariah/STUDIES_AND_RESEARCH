# Methodology

## Scope

This public case study recreates the measurement structure of a 2019 to 2021 beverage market-entry campaign in New York and New Jersey. It combines reported campaign parameters with reconstructed aggregate outcomes and scenario assumptions.

## Evidence labels

- Reported campaign parameter: recalled operational input from the campaign
- Reconstructed aggregate: recreated headline outcome based on campaign records and methodology
- Reconstructed estimate: approximate value used to show scale
- Portfolio assumption: modelling input used for demonstration
- Calculated: formula derived from labelled inputs

## Audience comparison

The portfolio model compares an education-led content approach with original brand-led content. The comparison illustrates how messaging performance would be evaluated. The public dataset does not contain user-level assignment records or a statistical significance test.

## Growth measures

Distributor growth uses the starting account count as the denominator.

```text
(11 outcome accounts - 3 starting accounts) / 3 starting accounts = 266.7%
```

The retail-location value is approximate. The analysis presents the numerical increase rather than implying audited precision.

## ROI model

Total investment equals monthly investment multiplied by campaign duration.

```text
$10,000 × 24 months = $240,000
```

The estimated three-year ROI uses discounted revenue from the case-study scenario.

```text
($380,000 - $240,000) / $240,000 = 58.3%
```

## Quality checks

- Total investment must equal monthly investment multiplied by duration.
- Net new accounts must equal outcome accounts minus baseline accounts.
- Distributor growth must equal 266.7% after rounding.
- Education-led engagement must exceed comparison engagement.
- ROI must equal 58.3% after rounding.

## Limitations

- Original platform exports and transaction records are not public.
- The audience comparison is reconstructed and does not prove causality.
- Revenue is a scenario estimate rather than an audited result.
- Retail availability is approximate.
- External effects such as the pandemic, seasonality, and distributor policy are not isolated.

## Recommended measurement plan

1. Assign unique campaign and retailer identifiers.
2. Track first order, reorder, and account retention separately.
3. Record tasting attendance and coupon redemption by location.
4. Use randomized or matched-market tests for message comparison.
5. Report gross margin and incremental profit alongside revenue.
