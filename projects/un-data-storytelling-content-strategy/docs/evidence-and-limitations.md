# Evidence and limitations

## Evidence basis

This case study draws from:

- A January 2024 internal project brief describing a 120-hour data analytics and content strategy program
- Original Tableau image exports dated February 2024
- A project case record documenting the intern's research, dashboard, content-planning, and reporting contributions

## Verified contributions

- Tableau visualizations covering language, education, tourism, destinations, and social-media interest
- An SDG dashboard using public UN datasets, with work covering SDGs 4, 5, 10, and 13
- Research on endangered Indigenous languages using UNESCO and UN public sources
- A data-informed content calendar
- Review of reach, saves, shares, and comments
- Findings and recommendations reported to the program director

## Source boundaries

- The project brief identifies Tableau, Python or SQL, content scheduling tools, and Google Analytics where applicable. The brief describes the team toolkit and does not prove that every participant used every listed tool.
- The brief states 120 total hours, while its detailed allocations total 110 hours. The repository flags this difference.
- The Tableau exports retain visible citations to their data sources. The source datasets are not republished.

## Excluded claims

The repository does not claim verified membership growth, engagement growth, reactivation rates, time savings, or institutional adoption. Those claims require separate outcome evidence.

## Privacy

No names of members, membership files, email addresses, internal communications, access credentials, or confidential UN records appear in this repository.
