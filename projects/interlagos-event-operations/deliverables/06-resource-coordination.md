# Furniture reuse across Formula 1 and Porsche Cup

## Project situation

Two major accounts at Interlagos. Tight budgets. Limited production and operational time.

## The operational decision

I coordinated the reuse of some furniture from the Formula 1 assignment for Porsche Cup. Sharing part of the furniture resource helped save money and time at the venue while supporting both accounts.

## Skills applied

| Skill | Application |
|---|---|
| Resource planning | Use some furniture across the two assignments |
| Cost management | Reduce spending through furniture reuse |
| Operational coordination | Connect resource allocation with delivery at the venue |
| Account management | Support two major accounts within constrained budgets and production time |

## Reusable planning framework

The fields below provide a template for explaining and planning a similar resource-sharing decision. They are not a completed historical inventory.

| Field | Planning purpose |
|---|---|
| Furniture item and quantity | Identify the resource under consideration |
| Requirement for each account | Establish where and when each account needs the resource |
| Availability and release | Check whether reuse fits both delivery requirements |
| Condition and suitability | Check readiness for the next use |
| Movement and handling | Plan any handling needed within the venue |
| Responsible lead | Establish ownership of the handoff |
| Cost and time comparison | Compare shared use with separate supply |

## Delivery takeaway

Managing two accounts together created an opportunity to share resources. The furniture decision linked commercial awareness with practical venue operations.
