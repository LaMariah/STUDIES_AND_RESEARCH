## Evidence categories

- Reported experience: personal account in the supplied portfolio, pending final wording review.
- Retrospective reconstruction: document rebuilt to explain the historical delivery approach.
- Demonstration template: new example showing how work would be structured today.
- Original evidence: reserved for permitted historical documents and photographs added later.
